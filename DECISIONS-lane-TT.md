| H481 | 2026-10-22 | **What a tooltip says.** One panel (`HHAbilityTip`): a gold key badge and the name; one numbers line (cooldown, wind-up, damage, plus duration / air time / reach / range where the ability has one); for Q the charge tiers ("Hold Q: tap 160 · 1.5 s: 288 · 3 s: 560, fires itself"); for the basic attack the string of three; the weapon's own `skills().text` (what it does); one authored line on what it is for (`HHAbilityInfo.FOR`, gold); one line on what the picked elements do on it (`ELEMENT_LINE`, tinted by the active element); and any upgrades slotted on that skill, in green with their live numbers. Kept to 4-6 lines. | current |
| H482 | 2026-10-22 | **Numbers are read live, never copied.** `HHAbilityInfo` reads every number at hover time: cooldowns from `HHWeapons.skills`, weapon numbers through the weapon's own `_t` (HHTune `<weapon>_*`), dash and potion from HHTune, damage through `HHFormulas.hit` (before the monster's resistance), the combo string through `HHWeapons.combo_hit`, the charge tiers through the weapon's `q_mult` and HHTune `charge_l2_s` / `charge_l3_s`, the stagger an upgrade buys through `HHFormulas.stagger_ticks`. Arcing Shot (a percent of max HP) shows the percent plus `HHFormulas.tile_damage` on `monster_hp` ("1.0% max HP (200 solo)"). Each value carries the HHTune key it came from (`sources`). An F1 retune shows in the next tooltip. | Claude, decided | "From the real numbers ... never hard-coded copies that can drift." The `sources` map lets a test prove every shown number is the tune value. | current |
