## Requests to the integrator (not edited here)

- R-B1 `src/game/fight.gd` `_placeholder()`: `var mi := MeshInstance3D.new()` is allocated before the player-body branch returns, so with `player_body.gd` present every fight leaks one MeshInstance3D ("1 ObjectDB instance was leaked at exit" on the smoke run). Move the allocation below the branch or `mi.free()` before `return body`.
- R-B2 `src/game/fight.gd`: call `player_body.bind(sim, player_id)` after `build()` so the body reads target / guard / windup for stance and aim (it copes unbound: `on_event` binds lazily on the first player event, but the first frames idle instead of wielding).
- R-B3 `src/core/tune.gd` / weapon kits: when daggers and bow kits land, their basic wind-ups should be 5 and 8 ticks (the clips' contact ticks) or the body will stretch / squash the anticipation to match (it clamps the rate to 0.5x-2x).
- R-B4 `HHSim`: a `land` event carries no air time; `leap {ticks}` does. Fine as is; noting the body depends on `leap.ticks`.
- R-B5 Lane A: when `art/weapons/weapons.json` lands, keep the B\|W keys (glb, hands, socket, offhand_socket, mount, second_hand, projectile, scale) and bake colour into COLOR_0; the body applies a lit vertex-colour material over whatever material the glb carries.
