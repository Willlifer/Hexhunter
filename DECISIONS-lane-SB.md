# DECISIONS, Lane SB (stagger bar): block H510-H519

The integrator folds these rows into DECISIONS.md. The author's ask (2026-10-22): "Combat feels pretty dang good, I would add the stagger bar, it should take 10 full water combos to stagger, and scale everything from that and see how it feels." Earlier (L-61): a monster CC / topple bar that water fills harder than any other element (H441: "water controls").

| H510 | 2026-10-22 | **The stagger bar (sim).** Every monster carries `sbar` (fill points), `sbar_need` (what topples it now), `sbar_idle`, `topples`, `toppled` (ticks left down): plain unit fields, so co-op snapshots and deltas carry them with no snapshot change, and all five are in `state_hash`. Feeds, all through `HHFormulas.stagger_fill(stagger, key, frac)` (a calc with a breakdown): a hunter's blow (`strike`: basics, Q/E/R hits, shots) = its stagger x `sb_hit_<the hunter's ACTIVE element>`; a hunter's telegraph (arcing shot, aftershock) the same; an action's reactions on the monster (`_land_reactions`) = the strongest of stagger x `sb_<reaction>` (geyser, steam, shatter, detonate, glint, eclipse, overheat, flux; a Swirl counts as the reaction it condensed into), x `sb_monster_trigger_frac` 0.25 when a monster or the arena set it off. No fill while toppled or immune; `sb_on` 0 switches it off. The per-hit stagger / flinch is untouched (`hurt()` unchanged): the bar is a layer on top. `sbar_feed(m, amount)` is public for later hooks. | Claude, decided | "I would add the stagger bar". Fill rides the stagger every hit and reaction already carries, so "how much it rocks the monster" and "how much it fills the bar" stay one number per source, scaled per element. | current |
| H511 | 2026-10-22 | **The anchor: one full water combo.** = a hunter with water ACTIVE at its attack spot on a still monster doing the weapon's whole basic string (lance / daggers: the 3-hit combo; the bow has no combo, H57, so 3 arrows), counted with every reaction its own water sets off on the monster (the Geysers: hit 3 lays water 2 + splash, so water 3 sits under the blows and the next water blows erupt). No skills, no dodging: reproducible. Measured by `tools/stagger_bar_calib.gd` (-> `design/stagger_bar.md`), from a fresh fight: lance 0.77 for the first string (no Geyser yet), then 2.77 per string (0.12 + 0.15 + 0.5 from the blows, two Geysers x 1.0); after 9 strings 22.93, after 10 25.70. Daggers: 0.64 then 2.64; 21.76 / 24.40. Bow: 0.36 then ~1.86 (one Geyser most strings); 15.24 / 17.60. **`sb_need` = 24.0**: 10 full water combos topple and 9 do not, for the lance (the anchor) AND the daggers; the bow needs ~14 of its 3-arrow strings (it has no string, and its arrows carry the basic stagger only). In seconds of string: lance 10 x 3.6 s = 36 s, daggers 10 x 2.0 s = 20 s, bow ~14 x 2.9 s. | Claude, decided | "it should take 10 full water combos to stagger". Basics + their own Geysers is the combo as played; skills differ per weapon and per upgrade, so they are fill on top, not part of the yardstick. 24 sits inside both melee weapons' 9..10 window, so the rule holds for both without a per-weapon threshold. | current |
| H512 | 2026-10-22 | **Topple.** A full bar topples the monster at once if it is grounded and surfaced (else on the first tick it is: a leap lands, a dive surfaces). For `sb_topple_s` 4.0 s: its brain does not run (no new attacks; EASY brain and NORMAL director both skipped), its wind-up and every telegraph of its own still waiting to land are cancelled (`telegraph_end { cancelled: true }`, knocked out of the attack), it stops and does not move, and it is **exposed** (H57's window reused: x`exposed_dmg_mult` 1.25 from every hunter) for the whole time. The bar drains from full to 0 over the topple; when it stands, `topples` +1 raises the next threshold. Events `topple { id, ticks, count, cause }`, `topple_end { id, need }`, and `monster_anim { clip: "stagger", topple: true }`. In-flight telegraphs are cancelled rather than left to land: the topple usually comes off a blow during a monster's wind-up, and knocking it out of that attack is the payoff. | Claude, decided | "When full: the monster topples ... no attacks, exposed". Reusing `exposed` keeps one punish rule, one HUD tag and one tune key. | current |
| H513 | 2026-10-22 | **The view.** HUD monster plate: a thin (6 px) water-blue bar under the HP bar, as wide (`HHHud.stagger_rect`, `stagger_frac`; the plate grows to hold it and the EXPOSED tag moves under it). On a topple the bar and its frame flash pale blue (state-driven, so a co-op mirror flashes too), the fill turns white-hot and pulses as it drains, and a small blue TOPPLED tag sits under its left end (EXPOSED stays at the right). Body: the sim emits the monster's existing `stagger` clip (all four have one); `HHMonsterBody.topple_hold` plays it down to its slump (the clip's key moment) and holds it there while `toppled` lasts, playing the recovery half only in the last part, so it stands as the sim lets it act. Renders: `tools/stagger_bar_preview.gd` -> `design/stagger_bar/` (sb_filling, sb_topple, sb_down, sb_up and x2 `_plate` crops). | Claude, decided | "Not busy": one thin bar and one tag while it matters; nothing on the arena floor. | current |
| H514 | 2026-10-22 | **The scale (H441).** Per point of stagger: blows by the ACTIVE element water 1.0, ice 0.6, thunder 0.5, wind 0.5, fire 0.35, dark 0.35, light 0.35; reactions Geyser 1.0, Steam 0.7, Shatter 0.6, Detonate 0.5, Eclipse 0.3, Glint 0.25, Overheat 0.35 and Flux 0.35 (both carry 0 stagger today, so they fill nothing). Per fill event: Geyser 1.0, Shatter 0.48, Steam 0.245, Detonate 0.175, Glint 0.125, Eclipse 0.105, a water basic 0.12, a fire basic 0.042. Per measured single-element combo (lance; daggers and bow alike): water 100 %, light 19 % (its Glints), ice 18 %, thunder and wind 15 %, fire and dark 10 %. Solo single-element strings never Shatter (ice needs a charged tile), so ice's real fill is the Shatter in mixed play: 0.48 a break, the most of any non-water source. All keys in `tune.gd`'s "stagger bar" section (F1 lists them together). | Claude, decided | "water most, then ice/shatter and geyser-like control, thunder detonations some, fire/dark/light little": water's lead is mostly its Geysers (2 per string), which is H441's "water controls" made literal. | current |
| H515 | 2026-10-22 | **Party.** `sbar_need` x (1 + `sb_party_mult` 0.6 per extra hunter), the same factor as monster HP (`party_hp_mult`): four hunters fill ~4x as fast against a x2.8 threshold, and the fight is ~2.8/4 as long, so a party sees about as many topples per fight as a solo hunter, not four times as many. Recomputed in `scale_for_party`. | Claude, decided | The brief: "scale with party_scale so 4 hunters don't topple 4x as often". | current |
| H516 | 2026-10-22 | **Rise and decay.** Each topple raises the next threshold by `sb_rise` 0.35 of the first (x1.35, x1.7, ...): a fight's first topple comes at 10 water combos, later ones need more. Unfed for `sb_decay_delay_s` 6 s, the bar drains `sb_decay_pct_s` 2 % of the threshold a second (a full bar would take 50 s): a steady string never decays (the lance's string feeds every 1.2 s), a party that walks away loses it slowly. | Claude, decided | Rising thresholds keep topples an event instead of a loop; a slow decay keeps "pressure built up" meaningful without letting a bar sit full forever. | current |
