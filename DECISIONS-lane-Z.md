| H90 | 2026-10-21 | Root cause of the blown-out web floor: in gl_compatibility a shadow-casting DirectionalLight3D is drawn in a second, additive pass, and each pass is tonemapped and sRGB-encoded on its own before they are summed (Godot's scene.glsl; the docs: "lights with shadows are rendered in sRGB space"). sRGB(ambient) + sRGB(sun) is far brighter and greener than sRGB(ambient + sun). Measured on the flat "round" arena: Compatibility matches Forward+ with the sun's shadow off (floor 183,198,143 vs 182,194,142) or with the ambient off (172 vs 174), and blows out to 240,249,215 only with both. Not the sky, the tonemapper, MSAA, the shadow mode, glow or our shaders (all tried). Fix: on the Compatibility renderer only, GKMaterials picks `*_web` variants of gk_surface / gk_surface_clear / gk_fade (render_mode ambient_light_disabled + shaders/gk_web_light.gdshaderinc), whose light() adds the ambient, the sky reflection and the distance fog inside the sun's call, so everything is summed in linear space and tonemapped once. HHWebLook (src/game/web_look.gd) turns the arena's Environment into the hh_web_* shader globals (called from HHArenaStage.sky and the arena view's fallback sky) and switches the engine's own fog off on the web (it is drawn in the first pass and would add a pale veil). Desktop never loads the variants (render diff of the Forward+ halves, round 1 vs final: MAE 0.1-0.2, caldera 1.1 from flame animation). | Claude, decided | The brief: branch on the renderer, desktop look unchanged. Disabling sun shadows on the web would have matched colours but lost every tree and cliff shadow. | current |
| H91 | 2026-10-21 | The web ambient is an estimate (three irradiance colours, up / side / down, from the procedural sky's gradient), fitted against Forward+ renders of all five arenas: sky estimate x0.72 (SKY_FIT), a flat colour ambient (the cave) x1.3 (COLOR_FIT); sky reflection x0.5. Tunables in HHTune: web_ambient, web_reflection, web_fog, web_glow_whiten, web_glow_gain (web only). Mean absolute pixel error, Compatibility vs Forward+, round 1 -> final: round 62 -> 5.6, pond 29.7 -> 9.2, caldera 23.0 -> 9.8, cave 14.6 -> 10.2, glade 39.8 -> 9.4. Sheets: design/web/round1_*.png and final_*.png (Forward+ left); `python tools/web_compare.py design/web <tag>` remakes them. | Claude, decided | Three review rounds; numbers seen, sheets looked at. | current |
| H92 | 2026-10-21 | Two more web-only fixes. (a) project.godot `rendering/limits/opengl/max_lights_per_object` 8 -> 24 and `max_renderable_lights` 32 -> 64 (opengl-only limits, Forward+ ignores them): the caldera floor is one mesh under many vent lights, and past 8 the vents in shadow went dark. (b) GK `blend: add` light pools (torch pools, lava glow) use shaders/gk_glow_web.gdshader on the web: on desktop the pool adds linear light under Filmic and bleaches toward warm white; on the web it is added after its own tonemap and stays a saturated orange smear. The web sheet pulls toward warm white by web_glow_whiten 0.45 x web_glow_gain 1.1 (cave wanted ~0.65, caldera ~0.3: a compromise). | Claude, decided | Seen in the cave and caldera sheets. | current |
| H93 | 2026-10-21 | Still different on the web, honestly: (1) the hunter renders darker in Compatibility even unshaded (its vertex colours come out as if sRGB-decoded; Forward+ with vertex_color_is_srgb on matches the web look): Lane Y's player_body material code, not changed here; the likely fix is converting the hunter mesh's colour array with linear_to_srgb when HHWebLook.active(). Monsters look the same in both. (2) Alpha-blended FX (fire paint tiles, telegraphs, steam) blend in sRGB on the web, so the fire tiles' soot reads darker and more opaque and caldera steam fainter; not compensated (bright-over-dark and dark-over-bright need opposite corrections). (3) The GK grass noise reads a little blockier on the web. (4) Caldera vent rims glow less (no HDR bloom on the web), the cave's torch pools are slightly smaller, the glade's sunlit patches a touch less contrasty. (5) StandardMaterial objects (hunter, monsters, emit roles) still get the sRGB-add brightening from H90, only GK surfaces are fixed. (6) The ambush card's cave walls are more saturated (its own spot light). Lobby, results and world map read the same. | Claude | Logged so the next pass knows what is left. | open |
