## Requests (to the integrator, Lane P)

- HHMonsterBody: a per-role material tint (`set_role_tint("crystal", Color)` or an emission driven from `u.cold`) so the crystals go smoky grey -> pale cyan; the fight reads `u.cold` each frame. Nothing else in the body is needed (the arm bones reuse the leg names).
- Arena kinds the brain reads: `shade`, `vent` (cold spots), `rock`/`pillar` (impassable, freeze_pillar targets), `lava` (never pathed onto). Lane C's cave generator should emit them by those names; `allowed_kinds()` = floor, shade, vent, sun, water.
- Ice as slow terrain: HHCharge.move_mult does not slow on a stasis marker or glaze; the chilled ground slows players only through the `slow` status in the payloads. If the author wants frosted hexes themselves to slow, that is a core rule (Lane P).
- Telegraph payloads carry `name` (`mist_wide`, `frost_beam_light`) for the cause string; the beam issues two telegraphs on one tick (ice and light halves), the view should draw both.
- `freeze` is a status the sim already treats as paralysis (`_paralysed`); a HUD icon for it, and `slow` lasting 8 s, are Lane P's to show.
- fight.gd's `_body_react` names (hit_react, stagger, death) match this clip set.
