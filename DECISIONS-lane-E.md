## Requests to the integrator (Lane P)

- `HHSim.telegraph(owner_id: String, hexes: Array, ticks: int, payload: Dictionary) -> void`: store `{ owner, hexes, ticks_left, payload }`; the view draws the hexes as danger shapes; when `ticks_left` hits 0 call `HHSwampfang.apply_payload(self, owner, hexes, payload)` (static) and emit `telegraph_end`. A payload with `ripple: true` or `dmg_pct == 0` is a marker only. The brain already branches on `sim.has_method("telegraph")`.
- `HHSim.monster_move(u: Dictionary, goal_hex: Vector2i) -> void`: path `u` toward `goal_hex` over hexes whose `kind` is in `u.allowed_kinds`, keeping the whole footprint (`HHHex.area(h, u.size)`) on such hexes; set `u.path`, reset `u.progress` only when not mid-step. The brain branches on `sim.has_method("monster_move")` and falls back to `sim.arena.path` (walkable hexes only).
- `HHSim._walk`: honour `u.walk_ticks` (float, ticks per hex) when present, instead of the global `walk_ticks` / `run_ticks`; the brain sets 14 for Swampfang.
- `HHSim.step`: after `_orders`, `for id in brains: brains[id].tick(self, units[id])`, with `var brains := {}` on HHSim. `HHFight.setup` creates `HHSwampfang.new("swampfang")` and an `HHMonsterBody` child, plays `monster_anim` events on the body (`body.play(e.clip, bool(HHSwampfangClips.CLIPS[e.clip].loop))`) and sets `body.rotation.y = u.facing`, `body.position = u.pos`.
- Targeting and damage: skip units with `u.untargetable == true`; multiply damage on a monster by `HHSwampfang.rear_mult(u, attacker_hex)` and log the x1.5 in the HHFormulas breakdown.
- `HHTune`: the integrator may mirror `HHSwampfang.TUNE` keys into `DEFAULTS` for the F1 panel; the brain reads `TUNE` directly today.
