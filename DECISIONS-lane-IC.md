# DECISIONS, Lane IC (ice readability), block H470-H479

Same row format as `DECISIONS.md`; the integrator folds these rows in on merge and marks H366 superseded in part there.
The author's playtest note that opened the lane: "Ice was horrible to play around. Visually very difficult to say when you are going to shatter it. And if you couldn't shatter it you could end up stuck in place. My friend thought it was an enemy mechanic. However it was very powerful when used before a monster attack."

| # | Date | Decision | By | Why | Status |
|---|------|----------|----|-----|--------|
| H470 | 2026-10-22 | **Hunters pass through crystals** (supersedes H366's wall and push-out). Root cause of the trap: `HHSim.blocked_for` made every crystal hex a wall for hunters (all but the hex underfoot) and only another element, a blow on a monster standing on it, or the monster's own crush could end one, so a ring of crystals round a hunter (ice cast over its own charged ground, Frozen Storm, a cast near the arena edge or the hull) left `arena.path` empty: every move order re-planned to nothing and the walk never left the hex for up to the crystal's 36 s; a crystal moat round the monster made walk-into-reach find no attack spot, so basics never started; and H366's push-out could slide a hunter whose own hex crystallised into a pocket further inside a crystal field. Now `blocked_for` adds no crystals for anyone, `_walk_hex` no longer re-routes round them, and `_crystal_safety` / `_open_hex_near` are gone (a crystal underfoot holds nobody, no `push_out` by "crystal"). Walking through a crystal never breaks it. Kept: the monster crushes crystals (H367, it never paths round them), a sweep beam stops at the first one (cover, H421 `is_block`), a hunter's blow on a crystal hex still shatters it (H365), Shatter numbers unchanged. EASY changes on purpose: `test_difficulty`'s EASY_BASELINE re-recorded for swampfang (seeds 1, 2) and rimeslithe (seeds 1, 2), the bot's fights there now walk through crystals (verified: with the old `sim.gd` and everything else of this lane the old hashes still pass); colambros and spirewalker unchanged. | Claude, decided | The author: "if you couldn't shatter it you could end up stuck in place". Of the options (crystals never form on a hunter / a boxed-in hunter may walk out / pass-through) only pass-through makes "never stuck" true by construction with no special cases, and the power the author liked ("very powerful when used before a monster attack") is the monster crush and the cover, both untouched. `tests/test_el_crystal_trap.gd` reproduces the trap (fails on the old sim: the hunter stays at (0, 0); the underfoot push lands it 3 hexes away; the moat blocks every hit) and passes now. | current |
| H471 | 2026-10-22 | **The shatter read.** `HHCharge.crystal_response(entry, element, steps)` returns what that element would do to a crystal (`shatter` / `feed` / `none`, the Shatter multiple it would fire, stored points, ice) by calling the very `_crystal_route` apply() uses (made static with `_shatter_plan`), and `crystal_mult(entry)` its size now; a test checks the preview against the real apply() for every element on several crystals. The view (`HHReactionFx`, `viewer` = the local hunter): every crystal carries a label with its size ("x7"); when the hunter's ACTIVE element would break it the crystal is PRIMED: the label adds "BREAK" in warm gold, the ice pulses warm (`crystal_primed_hz` 2.2) and glowing crack lines run up its faces (new `shaders/fx_crystal_crack.gdshader`, more cracks the more it stores); when only the backup would, the label says "W: BREAK"; a feed or nothing shows just the size. The ice glows brighter per stored point (`crystal_glow_per_point`). HUD: one line over the element pair for crystals within `crystal_hint_hexes` 7: "WATER SHATTERS x7" (pulsing gold, the biggest the active element would fire), else "W: FIRE SHATTERS x3", else a quiet "ICE FEEDS THE CRYSTAL". The shatter burst is sized by the multiple (more and faster shards, a white crack-light column, a wider gold ring) and floats "SHATTER xN". F1: `crystal_label` (0 hides the labels), `crystal_primed_hz`, `crystal_glow_per_point`, `crystal_hint_hexes`. Renders: `tools/crystal_read_preview.gd` -> `design/elements/crystal_read.png`, `crystal_pass.png`, `shatter_read.png`, `crystal_sheet.png`. | Claude, decided | "Visually very difficult to say when you are going to shatter it." The trouble was that the same element feeds one crystal and breaks the next (own axis and ice feed, past the cap or anything else breaks), so the tell has to be computed per crystal for the player's own elements, from the sim's rule, not drawn as a fixed look. One primed state (no colour scheme per element or owner) per the author's follow-up. View-only: no sim outcome changes. | current |
| H472 | 2026-10-22 | **No owner tint.** Party crystals and monster ice are not told apart by colour; the crystal look is unchanged apart from H471's read. | Author | The author dropped the goal: "Differentiating between player and enemy ice sounds hard as hell." The friend's "enemy mechanic" read most likely came from being trapped (H470). Rimeslithe makes frost (`glaze`, H421) and body / telegraph crystals, never an EL crystal, so in play every prism is the party's anyway. | current |
