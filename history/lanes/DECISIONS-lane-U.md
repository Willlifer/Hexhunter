## Requests (to Lane L / V / H, not edits)

- **R-U1 (Lane L, lobby):** the prep screen reads `HHSave.load_or_new().upgrades_for(weapon)` into `cfg.upgrades`, and `HHFight.setup_cfg` passes `cfg.upgrades` as the trailing argument of `sim.add_player`. Slotting UI: `save.slot(weapon, skill, id)` / `unslot`, max 3 per skill, `HHUpgrades.for_weapon(weapon)` lists the candidates, `HHUpgrades.name_of / blurb_of` for text. Include `t` (the potion) as a fifth slot column.
- **R-U2 (Lane H, results):** on `result == "win"`, `HHDrops.roll(cfg.monster, sim.rng, cfg.weapon, save.owned)`, then `save.own(id)` per drop, `save.record_hunt(...)`, `save.save()`. Rolling from `sim.rng` after the fight keeps the drop on the seed.
- **R-U3 (Lane V, HUD / view):** draw `u.immune > 0` as a ward on the hunter; draw `sim.projectiles` (each `{ hex, element, owner }`) as small moving markers and the `projectile` events as flashes; telegraphs named `aftershock` / `bomblet` are the hunter's own and may use the player telegraph tint.
