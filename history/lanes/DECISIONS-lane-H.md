# DECISIONS, Lane H (monster hull, change spec section 5), block H250-H259

Rows in DECISIONS.md format for the integrator to fold in. A second Lane H on 2026-10-21; the hunt-loop rows above are an earlier lane. Numbers H250-H259 as assigned by the coordinator (PLAN section 6 lists H250-H299 under Lane D Maps; Lane D used H240-H249).

Rows this work supersedes in part (the integrator marks them):
- **H338**: still true while a monster MOVES (it glides over a hunter); once it stands still the hunter is slid out (H251, H253), so no brain is left facing a hunter inside its footprint.
- **H344** (last sentence): a squashed hunter no longer stays inside the footprint; it is slid to the Attack Ring on the landing tick, after the squash's own damage. The cones' `min_dist 1` is harmless and stays.
- **H880** (last sentence): a monster body that moves into a hunter's path no longer stops the walk; the hunter re-routes round the hull (H254). Terrain walls still stop it.
- **H324** ("a leap lands it on the player"): it still lands where the player stood; the player is slid to the ring (H253).
