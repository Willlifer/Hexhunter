# DECISIONS, Lane P block (H271-H279)

Rows for the integrator to fold into DECISIONS.md. Same format. They build the author's change requests H268 (push-out hurts), H269 (leaps splash) and H270 (fainter range ring, thicker hull outline).

Rows this work supersedes in part (the integrator marks them):
- **H253**: push-out is no longer "not a hit": it hurts (H268, H271-H272, H277). Hunters gain three plain-data fields (`under`, `struck`, `pushed`), so "no new unit fields" no longer holds. The slide itself, its order and its target hex are unchanged.
- **H255**: the hull's ink band is 0.28 m inside / 0.16 m outside the border with a 0.12 m rim (was 0.24 / 0.14 / 0.11; H279).
- **H56** (range rings): the reach ring's opacity is `range_ring_alpha` 0.12 (daggers x0.75), was 0.62 (daggers 0.45; H278).
- The leap rows of the monster lanes (Colambros leap_stomp 26%, Spirewalker leaping_squash 28%): their landing damage is now the push-out's (H275, H276).
