# DECISIONS, Lane M block (H400-H419)

Rows for the integrator to fold into DECISIONS.md. Same format. They build the author's change spec sections 7 (enrage, element pulse, dot_pain_meter and purge, active avoidance) and 8 (set-pieces), every one of them NORMAL and up.

The block: the brief asked for H330-H359 "if free". In DECISIONS.md H330-H349 are Lane E's Rimeslithe and Spirewalker rows and H350-H357 are Lane F's audio rows, so this lane took the next free 30 (H400-H389). Only H400-H419 are used.

Every [FILL] in the spec was filled with a proposal. Those rows say "Claude, overturn freely". The numbers are F1 keys `tier_<key>` (`HHTierBrain.TUNE`, registered through `HHTune.register`), so `src/core/tune.gd` was not edited.

Rows this work supersedes in part (the integrator marks them):
- **H240** and guide/02 "Today NORMAL == EASY": NORMAL now differs (spec 7, 8). EASY stays identical (H417).
- **H246**: `test_normal_plays_like_easy_today` is replaced by `test_easy_matches_the_recorded_baseline` and `test_normal_differs_from_easy` (H417).

| H400 | 2026-10-21 | The NORMAL layer is one director, `HHTierBrain` (`src/monster/tier_brain.gd`, node-free). It has two hooks.
