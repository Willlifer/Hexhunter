| H880 | 2026-10-20 | Fix: a hunter's auto-walk (or a long move) could stall forever. ## Requests

- **R-S1 (Lane C, `src/game/fight.gd` and HUD):** add every `cfg.players` hunter with `sim.add_player(id, arena.spawns.player[0], ...)`: the sim seats them (H876) and scales monster HP (H877), so `_add_monster` needs no change. A downed hunter has `hp == 0` and `downed == true`: draw it downed (not dead) and the revive ring from `u.revive / HHTune.value("revive_ticks")` (or the `revive_progress` event's `frac`); `death` now only fires for the last hunter or solo. New events: `downed`, `revive_progress`, `revive_interrupted`, `revived`, `party_scale`.

No Callables were added. A mirror never steps, so it never runs `_revives` or `monster_target`; the HUD reads the fields.
- **R-S3 (integrator, `guide/02-core-contracts.md`):** HHSim's events list gains the five above; the Phase 4 paragraph should say the sim's `over` stays `"dead"` (HHFight shows "fallen"), hunters pass through each other, and no friendly fire (H882).
