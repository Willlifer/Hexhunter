## Requests to the integrator

- R1 (GKMaterials): merge `HHCaveGen.ROLES` (cave_floor, cave_wall, stalagmite, cave_boulder, frost, torch_flame, torch_post), `HHGladeGen.ROLES` (glade_grass, sun_grass, canopy, glade_trunk, glade_flower, bush) and `HHCalderaGen.ROLES` (basalt, ash, snow, lava, lava_crust, vent_rim, scoria) into `GKMaterials.ROLES`; the generators switch to them automatically (`HHGenUtil.role`). `lava` and `torch_flame` would benefit from an emissive key if the surface shader ever grows one.
- R2 (boot `--arena <id>`): load `arenas/cave.json`, `glade.json`, `caldera.json` through `HHGenUtil.from_dict` (keeps `extras` and `authored`), or make `HHArena.from_dict` keep unknown cell keys and an `authored` field.
- R3 (boot/fight): apply `arena.authored` (today `arena.spawns["authored"]`, rows `{ q, r, h, v, marker, glaze }`) through `sim.charge.author(Vector2i(q, r), h, v, marker)` at fight start; rows with `glaze: true` also need `sim.charge.entries[hex].glaze` set (a permanent glaze), which `author` has no parameter for.
- R4 (HHArena): add `authored: Array` to HHArena and `to_dict`/`from_dict`; drop the `spawns["authored"]` stand-in.
- R5 (HHCharge.can_hold): refuse kinds `pillar` and `tree` as well as `rock` (`HHGenUtil.NO_CHARGE`).
- R6 (sim): a pillar cell at 0 hp flips to `kind_after` and `walkable = true`; vents re-form their authored fire 1 after a decay (the generator only marks them).
- R7 (HHTune): `pillar_hp` (300 today in `HHCaveGen.PILLAR_HP`) belongs in HHTune.
- R8 (bench wiring): `HHBench.open(tree)` now lists four generators; nothing else changes for `--bench`.
