## Requests

- **R-FX1 (fight.gd, integrator):** in `HHFight._process`, beside `sfx.on_event(e, sim)`, add `if view != null: view.on_event(e, sim)`. The HHSfx relay covers it until then (H707); after it, the relay switches itself off.
- **R-FX2 (Lanes MA/MB, clips):** Colambros' `ember_spit` clip could raise the head through the wind-up (the brief's "head raised"); the effects already put the glow on the `mouth` socket, so a raised head lifts the glow with it. Spirewalker's `leaping_squash` crouch could fold the reeds (the brief's "reeds folding"); not done here (pose is the clip's job). Keep the socket names `mouth`, `fist_l`, `fist_r`, `reeds`, `frill`, `back`, `hips_top`, `head_top`, `shoulder_l/r` and the bones `toe_hl` / `toe_hr`: the effects look them up by name.
- **R-FX3 (dev / Lane P):** `HHEnv` holds the sim and the sim holds `env`, a RefCounted cycle: every run now ends with "5 resources still in use at exit" (sim.gd, env.gd, arena.gd, charge.gd, the brain). Not a SCRIPT ERROR; a `weakref` or clearing `sim.env` on teardown fixes it.
