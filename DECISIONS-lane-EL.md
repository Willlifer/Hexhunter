Block H360-H389. H300-H329 (the block the brief named) is taken in DECISIONS.md by Lane E's monster rows (H300-H317, H320-H329; H330-H357 too), so this lane took the next free block of 30 (H358-H399 was free; rounded to H360). | H360 | 2026-10-21 | **Swirl** (spec 2.1, supersedes in part H2/H21's Gale: wind on charged ground). Containment first: Swirl cannot pull from a hex a reaction used or produced in this action (H361), a Swirl centre (and its landing) is not swirled again in the same action, and the burst it fires does not spread. The rule: wind on EMPTY ground still arms a gale marker, and an element landing on a gale marker still spreads to the ring (kept). Wind landing on PLAIN charge (charged, no marker, not a crystal, not frost, not the biome's permanent ground) pulls every plain charge within the pull radius of the cast's centre (the shape hex nearest the shape's mean) inward: per-element sums; fire and water both present with either at 2+ erupt as Steam (consumed = both, capped 6), light and dark the same as Eclipse, else opposites net; each axis capped at 3; laid on the centre, or the first free hex of its ring when the centre holds a marker, a crystal or permanent ground; pulled hexes clear. A condensed tier 3 fires that element's burst (Overheat / Geyser / Glint / Flux) once at the landing, no ring splash. At most ONE reaction at the centre: the biggest multiple wins (the other axis just keeps its capped charge). With no reaction the condensed charge spreads tier 1 of each element one ring outward (temporary gale copies, 13.5 s) - the "spread one ring outward" half of the spec. Pull radius [FILL]: 2 for a basic, a line or a band; 3 for a wide area (at least `swirl_area_min_hexes` 7 hexes filling 60%+ of the disk round its centre: Eruption, Arrowstorm, Exchange Nova). Crystals stay put (wind does nothing to them). | Claude, overturn freely (radius and the meet / net rule) | The spec's line-2 / area-3 split, measured from the cast's own shape so every weapon and upgrade gets it without a per-skill table. | H361 | 2026-10-21 | **Containment, as built** (spec 0.3). One ACTION = one caster's paints in one tick (`HHSim.paint` declares `HHCharge.begin_action("<tick>:<caster>")`; a weapon hit's Shatter, a crush and `react_at` declare their own). In an action every hex reacts at most once (`_reacted`), and every hex a reaction wrote to (Overheat's ring, a burst's splash, Shatter's fragments, a Swirl's landing, gale copies) is `_produced`: anything else landing on a reacted or produced hex in that action is laid like spread (it nets, fires no marker, bursts, erupts and shatters nothing; frozen hexes are skipped). A direct `HHCharge.apply` with no declared action is its own action (tests). All blasts of one action on one unit land together (H50's chain: strongest in full + 15% of each other; now with the strongest stagger of the list, not the strongest blast's). Consequence: a basic and a skill of one hunter landing in the same tick are one action. | Claude, decided | The spec's containment clause made into one mechanism every reaction uses, instead of a clause per reaction. | current |

[FILL] formula: `consumed x steam_mult_per_point` (1.0) x the trigger's auto attack, hex in full, ring `eruption_ring_frac` 0.5: water 2 + a fire basic = x3 (Overheat's), water 3 + fire 3 = x6. | H364 | 2026-10-21 | **Who gets hurt: ONE rule** (spec 3.3, DECIDE-C H262; supersedes in part H50's "reactions a monster or the arena sets off use a 40-damage unit" and extends H33 / H882). `HHSim.reaction_hit` is called by every reaction and eruption; nothing restates it.
